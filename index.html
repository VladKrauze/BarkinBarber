Services & Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing

Pricing is based on your dog's weight. Every dog is different — all listed prices are starting prices and may vary based on breed, coat condition, temperament, and the time required to safely complete the service. Final pricing is confirmed before grooming begins.

Bath & Brush

Premium shampoo and conditioner, thorough bath, blow dry, brush-out and removal of loose undercoat, nail grinding, ear cleaning, paw-pad cleanup, sanitary cleanup, and light trimming/outline cleanup as needed.

Full Groom

Everything in Bath & Brush plus a full haircut and styling based on your dog's breed and your personal request. Tailored, hand-finished results every visit.
